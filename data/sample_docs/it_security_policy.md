# Northwind Bank — IT Security Policy (fictional sample)

## Passwords
Passwords must be at least 14 characters long and are rotated every 90 days.
Multi-factor authentication is mandatory for email, VPN and all customer-data systems.

## Data handling
Customer data must never be copied to personal devices or uploaded to public AI tools.
Suspected security incidents must be reported to the Security Operations Centre within 1 hour of discovery.

## Devices
Company laptops must use full-disk encryption and lock automatically after 5 minutes of inactivity.
