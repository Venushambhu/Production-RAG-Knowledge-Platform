# Northwind Bank — Travel and Expense Policy (fictional sample)

## Submitting claims
Expense claims must be submitted within 30 days of the expense date through the finance portal, with an itemised receipt attached.
Claims above EUR 500 require approval from the department head.

## Travel
Economy class is required for flights under 6 hours. Business class may be booked for flights of 6 hours or more with director approval.
The daily meal allowance is EUR 45 for domestic travel and EUR 60 for international travel.

## Non-reimbursable items
Alcohol, personal entertainment, traffic fines and minibar charges are not reimbursed.
