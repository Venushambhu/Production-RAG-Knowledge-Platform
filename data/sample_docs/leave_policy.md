# Northwind Bank — Employee Leave Policy (fictional sample)

## Annual leave
Full-time employees receive 24 days of paid annual leave per calendar year.
Up to 5 unused days may be carried over into the first quarter of the next year; any remaining days expire on 31 March.

## Sick leave
Employees must inform their line manager before 10:00 on the first day of absence.
A medical certificate is required for absences longer than 3 consecutive working days.

## Parental leave
Primary caregivers receive 26 weeks of paid parental leave. Secondary caregivers receive 4 weeks.
Requests must be submitted through the HR portal at least 8 weeks before the planned start date.
